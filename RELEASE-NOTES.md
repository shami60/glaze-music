# What's new in 1.0.4

- YouTube Music now matches the real thing: **Mixed for you**, **New releases** and
  **Artists you follow** shelves, with the artists a mix was built from shown on each card.
- The YouTube Music view remembers where you were — tab, playlist and scroll — when you
  pop over to Home or Favourites and come back.
- Deleting a playlist from inside the playlist now asks for confirmation.
- Downloads in progress show a real percentage instead of a spinner.

## Fixes

- A mix could hang forever on "Loading playlist tracks…" (endless station continuations).
- Sidebar collapse no longer stutters on the Explore screen.
- Playlists open in the right view instead of the album view.
