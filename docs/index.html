Built for people who actually keep a music library

Not a website in a window. Playback, the index, downloads and lyrics all run locally.

One library, two sources

Point it at a folder and it indexes everything with SQLite — tags, artwork, albums, artists. Sign in to YouTube Music and your library comes with you: your liked songs and your playlists, right next to your own files.

  • Your liked songs and playlists from YouTube Music
  • Mixed for you, New releases, Artists you follow
  • One search across both — files and streaming

Lyrics that follow the vocal

Word-by-word highlighting paced by syllable count, not character length — so a long word gets the time it needs and a short one doesn't sit lit for a second.

  • Instrumental breaks no longer drag the highlight along
  • Right-to-left lyrics render correctly
  • Any line can be translated in place

A real 10-band equalizer

Ten bands from 32 Hz to 16 kHz, seven presets, and an honest label: change a band by hand and the control reads Custom instead of pretending you're still on a preset.

  • Presets survive a restart — the control reflects what's applied
  • 8D spatial audio, room reverbs, crossfade

Your friends can see what you're listening to

Glaze Music shows up on Discord — the track, the artist, the real cover art and a live progress bar. So when you're listening, your friends can see it too, right in Discord.

  • Live track, artist, cover art and progress — just like the screenshot
  • Pausing shows exactly where you stopped, seeking updates it instantly
  • No bot, no account linking, nothing sent to a server

Pin anything to the sidebar

Hover a playlist anywhere — YouTube Music, Explore, an artist page — and pin it. It comes back in the view it came from, not whichever tab happens to be active.

  • Works across every browsing surface
  • Remembers the context you pinned it from

A mini player for when the window is too much

Shrink the whole app to a narrow strip and keep it beside whatever else you're doing. Same player, not a cut-down version.

  • Full transport and the current position
  • Follows you across virtual desktops

Have a look around

Light and dark themes, five palettes, and a layout that fills the window. Scroll sideways — every shot is shown at its real size, never stretched.

Questions people actually ask

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no telemetry, no upsell, no ads. The source is public on GitHub.

Do I need a YouTube Music account?

No. The local library, playlists, favourites, equalizer, lyrics for local files and downloads all work offline. Only the streaming shelves need an account.

What audio quality does it stream?

Around 160 kbps, because the source is YouTube — there is no lossless stream to offer. Files in your own library always play at their native quality.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows “Unknown publisher”. Choose More info → Run anyway. Signing is the only reason for that warning.

Does it work on macOS or Linux?

Not today. The build targets Windows. The Electron code isn't Windows-specific, but nothing else is tested.

Where is my data?

In %APPDATA%\glaze-music on your machine. The index, playlists and settings never leave it.

How do updates work?

The app checks on launch and installs on quit, downloading only the parts that changed. You can also grab the newest installer below.
